## Preamble

The Hermetic tradition — attributed to Hermes Trismegistus, a syncretic figure merging the Greek Hermes with the Egyptian Thoth — is the oldest continuous philosophical framework in the Western world. Its core texts include the Corpus Hermeticum (~2nd century CE), the Emerald Tablet (~6th–8th century CE), and The Kybalion (1908, attributed to "Three Initiates," likely William Walker Atkinson).

The Kybalion codifies the tradition into seven principles. This document maps each principle against the UFFT mathematical framework. The Emerald Tablets of Thoth (Doreal text) are treated in a separate companion document; this analysis focuses on the classical Hermetic tradition and the Kybalion's systematisation of it.

The same classification system applies throughout.
