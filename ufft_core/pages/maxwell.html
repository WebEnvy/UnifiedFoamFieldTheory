Vacuum Equations

The Helmholtz decomposition splits any displacement field D into an irrotational component E (curl-free) and a solenoidal component B (divergence-free). The wave equation □D = 0 applied to this decomposition gives:

  • ∇ × E = −∂B/∂t — Faraday's law. The transverse wave coupling condition.

Charges are torsion defects (disclinations) in the foam lattice. By Volterra's theorem (1907), topological defects in an elastic medium have quantised, conserved charges whose flux through any closed surface equals the enclosed charge.
