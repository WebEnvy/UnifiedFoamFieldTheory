The Complete Schwarzschild Metric

The grr Discrepancy — Resolved

The covariant vacuum density derivation (Part XVII) gave gtt = −c²(1 − 2GM/rc²). The spatial metric component grr was listed as an open problem: the isotropic cell conservation law ρl³ = const gave grr = (1−x)−2/3, which differs from the Schwarzschild (1−x)−1.

The Resolution: Anisotropic Deformation

The equation of state P = ρc² gives Poisson ratio ν = 1/2 (relativistic incompressibility). This is the maximally stiff case — sound speed equals light speed.

Under a radial pressure gradient, ν = 1/2 requires the radial deformation to be exactly twice the angular deformation (the standard elasticity result: strain ratio = 1/ν). The original derivation assumed isotropic deformation (all directions equal). This was wrong.

With anisotropic deformation and mass conservation ρ × lr × l⊥² = const, the unique solution is α = 1/2:

grr = (1 − 2GM/rc²)−1
The Schwarzschild spatial metric — derived from foam incompressibility. Zero free parameters.

The complete Schwarzschild metric is now foam-derived: gtt from Part XVII, grr from ν = 1/2.
