From Foam to Atoms — The Particle Hierarchy

What every known particle and force IS in the foam — from Planck scale to periodic table

Standard physics has a zoo of particles, forces, and constants that are measured but not explained. UFFT says they all emerge from one substrate (Planck-scale foam) and one axiom (B + V = D). This Part walks from the smallest scale to the largest, explaining what each known entity physically IS in the foam.

Status labels used throughout:

  • DERIVED — mathematically proven from foam geometry, numerically verified
  • MECHANISM — physical process identified, qualitative match confirmed
  • IDENTIFIED — logical mapping to foam structure, consistent but not yet independently computed
  • OPEN — recognised as needed, not yet done

Level 0 — The Substrate (10⁻³⁵ m)

An infinite, pre-existing electrical foam at the Planck scale. Not created by the Big Bang — the Big Bang was a pressure wave in this foam.

Each foam bubble is a truncated octahedron (Kelvin cell) — the unique convex polyhedron that tiles 3D space with minimal surface area per unit volume. The foam adopts this geometry because it is the lowest-energy packing.

The numbers (all DERIVED ✓):

  • Cell symmetry: Oh (octahedral group, 48 elements)
  • V = 24 vertices, E = 36 edges, F = 14 faces (8 hexagonal + 6 square)
  • Planck density: ρ₀ = 5.155 × 10⁹⁶ kg/m³
  • Fine structure constant: α⁻¹ = 137.035999055 (0.21 ppb, zero free parameters)
B(x) + V(x') = D
Every displacement creates a bubble and a void. Neither exists without the other.

Level 1 — Forces (How Disturbances Propagate)

Gravity — DERIVED ✓

A density gradient in the foam. Mass compresses the foam around it, creating a pressure gradient. Objects move down the gradient — pushed by higher-pressure foam behind them.

ρ(r) = ρ₀(1 − 2GM/rc²) → Φ = −GM/r. Newton's law recovered exactly. No free parameters.

Electromagnetism — DERIVED ✓

Tension in the foam lattice. When the lattice is distorted (charged particle), the tension propagates as electromagnetic waves. The speed of light is the speed of a pressure wave through Planck-density foam: c = √(P₀/ρ₀).

Coupling strength α⁻¹ = 137.035999055 — derived from Oh representation theory. Published: 10.5281/zenodo.19011758

The Strong Force — MECHANISM ✓

Torsion (twisting) of the foam lattice. The foam cell has three independent axes it can twist around — these are the three colour charges (red, green, blue). Colour neutrality (r + g + b = white) is the torsion closure condition: total twist returns to zero.

  • 8 gluons = 8 independent torsion generators of SU(3)
  • Confinement: torsion potential V(θ) = k(1−cos θ) → constant force at large distances
  • Asymptotic freedom: same potential is harmonic at θ → 0 → coupling vanishes at short range

The Weak Force — MECHANISM ✓

Each foam discharge event creates a right-handed helical structure. Left-handed particles couple to this; right-handed particles don't. This IS parity violation — geometric, not postulated.

  • W± = torsion-discharge states coupled to EM foam
  • Z⁰ = pure torsion-discharge, no EM coupling
  • W/Z masses: OPEN

The Gauge Group — DERIVED ✓

SU(3) × SU(2) × U(1) emerges from the torsion topology of the foam cell. SU(3) from three torsion axes. SU(2) from chiral discharge coupling. U(1) from displacement propagation. The Standard Model gauge group, derived from the geometry of a single cell.

Level 2 — Particles (Stable and Unstable Excitations)

Particles are stable topological structures in the foam — quantised vortices, knots, or standing displacement waves that persist because the foam's topology protects them.

Photon — DERIVED ✓

A transverse pressure wave propagating through the foam at c = √(P₀/ρ₀). Massless because the dispersion relation is linear. A photon is a sound wave in the foam. The foam is just incredibly stiff (Planck pressure), so the wave moves at c.

Electron — IDENTIFIED

The simplest stable charged topological defect in the foam. A self-sustaining vortex or knot carrying one unit of U(1) charge. Stable because you can't smooth out the knot without cutting it — topological protection. This is why electrons don't decay.

Electron mass: OPEN (requires knot classification programme).

Muon & Tau — IDENTIFIED

Higher-energy versions of the electron's topological defect. Three generations from the B-V-D triadic structure:

  • Electron (1st gen): B-topology dominant — lowest energy
  • Muon (2nd gen): V-topology dominant — intermediate energy, decays in 2.2 μs
  • Tau (3rd gen): D-topology dominant — highest energy

Mass ratios: OPEN. The Koide parameter θ is determined (= α in angular coordinates), but individual masses require knot classification.

Neutrinos — IDENTIFIED

Chiral foam modes carrying no electric charge. They couple to the helical weak discharge but not to lattice tension (EM) or torsion (strong). Nearly massless because their topological structure is the simplest possible. Three flavours map to the same B/V/D triadic structure. Oscillations = mode conversion during propagation.

Quarks — IDENTIFIED

Bound bubble-void clusters carrying both fractional electric charge AND colour charge. Composite foam structures with a displacement knot (giving charge) wrapped in a torsion winding (giving colour).

Fractional charges (⅓, ⅔) arise from torsion partially screening the displacement charge. Quarks can't exist alone — isolated colour charge means unclosed torsion extending to infinity, costing infinite energy.

Gluons — MECHANISM ✓

Propagating torsion waves — quantised twists carrying colour charge between quarks. 8 types = 8 generators of SU(3). Massless (linear dispersion). Self-interacting because twists in one direction affect stiffness for twists in other directions — the physical origin of non-Abelian gauge theory.

Proton & Neutron — IDENTIFIED

Three quarks locked together by colour-neutral torsion. Proton (uud): extraordinarily stable (lifetime > 10³⁴ years) — the lightest baryon, nothing lighter to decay into. Neutron (udd): slightly heavier, decays when free (≈ 15 min), stable inside nuclei.

W±, Z⁰ Bosons — MECHANISM (masses OPEN)

Torsion-discharge excitations — combined twist-and-discharge events coupling to left-handed particles. Massive because the discharge structure has a characteristic energy scale. Computing the actual masses (80.4 GeV, 91.2 GeV) requires the full coupling derivation.

Higgs Boson — OPEN

In UFFT, mass comes from topological complexity. The Higgs field would correspond to the foam's resistance to topological deformation — the stiffness that determines how much energy each knot configuration stores. The Higgs mass (125 GeV) and its couplings have not been derived from foam geometry.

Level 3 — Composite Objects (10⁻¹⁵ to 10⁻¹⁰ m)

Nuclei

Clusters of protons and neutrons held by residual strong force — torsion leaking between colour-neutral nucleons, analogous to van der Waals forces between neutral atoms.

Atoms — DERIVED (framework) ✓

A nucleus (compressed void cluster creating a foam density gradient) surrounded by electrons (topological defects in quantised standing-wave patterns). The Schrödinger equation, Born rule, double-slit interference, and complementarity are all derived from foam wave mechanics.

Bohr radius and Aufbau ordering: OPEN.

What's Honestly Open

The particle spectrum problem — computing which topological knots are stable and what their masses are — is the major unfinished programme. UFFT explains what particles ARE and derives their symmetry properties (gauge groups, α, confinement, parity violation), but does not yet compute individual masses from first principles. That requires the knot classification programme and the covariant vacuum density (the sole remaining hard open problem).

The framework is: substrate → symmetry → forces → particles. The first three levels are derived. The fourth is mapped but not yet computed.
