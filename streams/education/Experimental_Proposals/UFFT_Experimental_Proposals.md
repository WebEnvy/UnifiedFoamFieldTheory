### Proposal A — Three-Particle Entanglement Cascade

**UFFT Prediction 3:** For a three-photon GHZ-class state prepared via cascaded parametric down-conversion, the tripartite correlator is:

⟨X ⊗ X ⊗ X⟩ = −1

**What falsifies it:** ⟨X⊗X⊗X⟩ ≠ −1 at >3σ.

**Proposed test:** Any quantum optics laboratory with a three-photon entanglement source. The measurement is a standard Pauli-X correlation on three spatially separated detectors. Groups at Vienna (Zeilinger), Hefei (Pan), Waterloo, or Brisbane (White) have demonstrated three-photon GHZ states. The additional measurement (X⊗X⊗X on the specific cascade geometry) requires no new hardware — only a deliberate choice of measurement basis and state preparation protocol.

**Estimated cost:** Negligible (beam time on existing apparatus).
**Timeline:** Weeks, once a collaborator is engaged.

### Proposal B — Neutron Electric Dipole Moment

**UFFT Prediction 8:** The neutron EDM is exactly zero:

d_n = 0

In UFFT, CP violation arises entirely from the foam topology (the baryon asymmetry η = α³/(F_hx × C_A⁴)), not from a θ-term in QCD. There is no strong CP problem because there is no θ. The neutron EDM vanishes as a theorem, not as a fine-tuning coincidence.

**What falsifies it:** Any nonzero d_n at any confidence level.

**Current status:** The nEDM experiment at PSI (Paul Scherrer Institute, Switzerland) currently sets |d_n| < 1.8 × 10⁻²⁶ e·cm (90% CL). The n2EDM upgrade (operational 2025–2026) targets 10⁻²⁷ e·cm. The SNS nEDM experiment at Oak Ridge targets 10⁻²⁸ e·cm by ~2030.

**Proposed action:** No experimental proposal needed — these experiments are already running. UFFT's prediction (exact zero) should be published before their next results so it is on record as a prior prediction, not a post-hoc fit.

**Timeline:** n2EDM results expected 2026–2027. SNS nEDM results ~2030.

**What falsifies it:** An independent measurement confirming the Rb value at >3σ, or any measurement excluding α⁻¹ = 137.035999055 at >3σ.

**Current status:** The LKB group (Paris) is preparing an independent Rb measurement. Berkeley is refining the Cs measurement. Several groups are pursuing alternative approaches (molecular spectroscopy, electron g-2 comparison).

**Proposed action:** Publish the UFFT α prediction with its full derivation (already in Papers #1–#2) and explicitly state the Cs/Rb discrimination before the next precision measurement reports. This establishes UFFT as the only framework that takes a definite side in the α tension.

### Proposal D — Neutron Star Sound Speed

**UFFT Prediction 10:** The speed of sound in neutron star cores exceeds the conformal limit:

c_s > c/√3

This arises because the BCC foam lattice in the high-density regime (where cells are maximally compressed) has a face Laplacian sound channel whose velocity is set by the T₂g eigenvalue: c_s = c√(7/12) ≈ 0.764c > c/√3 ≈ 0.577c.

**What falsifies it:** Confirmed c_s < c/√3 in any neutron star core.

**Current status:** NICER (Neutron Star Interior Composition Explorer, on ISS) measures neutron star radii via X-ray pulse profiling. Combined with gravitational wave data from LIGO/Virgo merger events (GW170817 etc.), equation-of-state models can constrain the core sound speed. Current data already suggests c_s > c/√3 in massive neutron stars, but with large uncertainties.

**Proposed action:** A focused analysis paper comparing UFFT's predicted c_s = c√(7/12) against the latest NICER+LIGO constraints on the neutron star equation of state. This requires no new data — only a careful Bayesian comparison with published posteriors.

**Timeline:** Feasible immediately with published data. NICER continues to accumulate data through 2026+.

## Priority 2: Predictions Testable Within a Decade

### Proposal E — CP Phase Ratio (DUNE)

**UFFT Prediction 9 (the sharpest test):**

δ_PMNS / δ_CKM = 3 exactly

The ratio of the leptonic to hadronic CP-violating phase equals the colour number C_A = 3. This is exact — it depends on nothing but the number of hexagonal faces of the Kelvin cell divided by its colour factor.

Current values: δ_PMNS = 197° ± 25° (T2K + NOvA), δ_CKM = 66.2° ± 3.4° (CKMfitter). The ratio 197/66.2 ≈ 2.98, consistent with 3 at 0.1σ. But the PMNS error bars are too large for a definitive test.

**What falsifies it:** δ_PMNS/δ_CKM ≠ 3 at >3σ, once both phases are measured to sufficient precision.

**DUNE (Deep Underground Neutrino Experiment):** Under construction at Sanford Lab, South Dakota. First beam expected ~2029. DUNE will measure δ_PMNS to ~10° precision, which combined with CKM precision will test the ratio = 3 prediction at ~3σ sensitivity.

**Proposed action:** Submit a brief paper to Physical Review D or a letter to Physics Letters B stating the prediction and the specific DUNE measurement that tests it. The paper should be self-contained: define the prediction, state the current experimental values, compute the projected sensitivity, and identify the falsification criterion. No UFFT jargon needed — frame it as "a geometric prediction for the CP phase ratio."

**Timeline:** Paper can be written now. DUNE first oscillation results ~2031–2033. Definitive test ~2035.

**UFFT Prediction 5:** No dark matter particle will ever be detected. The dark matter ratio Ω_DM/Ω_b arises from BCC cell anisotropy — it is a geometric property of the foam, not a new particle species.

**Proposed action:** UFFT's prediction is already on record (Zenodo papers). Continue monitoring. Each null result from direct detection, collider searches, and indirect detection strengthens the prediction.

**Timeline:** Ongoing. LZ final results ~2028. Generation-3 experiments (DARWIN/XLZD) ~2030s.
