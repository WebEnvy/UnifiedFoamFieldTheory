# Visual Brief — Quark Confinement in the Foam

**For:** AI image generation (Gemini / Midjourney / DALL-E)
**Style:** Educational poster — clear, labelled, dramatic but accurate
**Background:** Dark (#0a0e17 or similar deep navy/black)

---

## What to show

A **Y-junction** of four truncated octahedra (Kelvin cells) viewed at a 3/4 angle:

### Centre cell — The Junction
- One truncated octahedron at the centre (24 vertices, 36 edges, 14 faces: 8 hexagons + 6 squares)
- Semi-transparent with a warm **golden glow** on three of its hexagonal faces — these are the T₂g flux tube connections
- The other 5 hex faces are dim/dormant
- Small glowing gold point at the very centre of this cell

### Three quark cells — branching outward
- Three more truncated octahedra connected to the junction cell, each sharing one hexagonal face with it
- Arranged at roughly 120° apart (actually tetrahedral angles — body diagonals of a cube)
- Each cell is tinted in its **quark colour**:
  - **Red** cell (up quark) — upper right
  - **Green** cell (up quark) — lower left  
  - **Blue** cell (down quark) — lower right
- The shared hex face between each quark cell and the junction glows **gold** (this is the colour field / gluon)
- The quark cells' other hex faces glow faintly in their respective colour (this is the T₁u fermion mode)

### Flux tubes
- Between the junction and each quark cell, a subtle **golden energy tube** or glowing thread passes through the shared hex faces
- These represent T₂g colour flux tubes — the strong force
- They should look like contained energy channels, not free-floating lines

### The stretch (optional but powerful)
- If showing string breaking: the red quark cell is pulled away from the junction
- 2-3 additional cells fill the gap between junction and red (the flux tube extending)
- At the break point: a bright flash with a **cyan** cell (new antiquark) and a **magenta** cell (new quark) appearing
- The flux tube between junction and red is broken/faded

## Geometry reference

The truncated octahedron has:
- 24 vertices
- 36 edges  
- 14 faces: 8 hexagons + 6 squares
- The hexagons sit at the 8 body-diagonal corners (normals pointing toward ±1,±1,±1)
- The squares sit at the 6 axis-aligned positions (top, bottom, front, back, left, right)
- Neighbouring cells share hexagonal faces along body diagonals at spacing (2a, 2a, 2a) where a is the half-edge length

The cells should look like real truncated octahedra — not simplified hexagons or spheres. The wireframe edges should be visible. The faces should be semi-transparent with the colour showing through.

## What NOT to do

- Don't use spheres for quarks — quarks ARE the cell face vibrations
- Don't show gluons as wiggly lines between point particles — they're face-to-face displacement patterns
- Don't make it look like a Feynman diagram — this is physical geometry, not abstract notation
- Don't simplify the truncated octahedron into a sphere or cube — the 14-face structure (8 hex + 6 square) is the whole point
