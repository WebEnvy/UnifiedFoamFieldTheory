Y-junction topology: The junction cell at the centre is where three T₂g flux tubes meet. Each tube connects to one quark through a shared hexagonal face.

The gold glow on shared faces is the colour field — gluons. It oscillates at √7 ≈ 2.65× the base frequency.

Pull the red quark to see confinement in action. Energy rises linearly with distance (V = σr). At ~1 GeV the string snaps and a new quark-antiquark pair materialises from the vacuum — you can never isolate a single colour charge.
5 dormant neighbours surround the junction — they share hex faces too, but T₂g only flows along the three colour channels. That selectivity is what makes exactly 3 colours, not 8.
