Planck scale

10⁻³⁵ m — One cell

One truncated octahedron. 14 faces. Every particle, every force, every constant of nature comes from vibrations of this cell.

Nuclear scale

10⁻¹⁵ m — T₂g confinement

~10²⁰ cells. T₂g colour confinement binds quarks into protons and neutrons. The strong force can't reach further than this.

Atomic scale

10⁻¹⁰ m — A₁g binding

~10²⁵ cells. A₁g electromagnetic binding holds electrons to nuclei. Chemistry begins. Everything you can touch exists at this scale.

Human scale

1 m — 10³⁵ cells

You are approximately 10³⁵ vibrating Kelvin cells. Every atom in your body runs the same eigenmode patterns from Lecture 2.

Stellar scale

10¹⁰ m — A₁g gravity

~10⁴⁵ cells. A₁g gravitational collapse compresses matter into a star. The same breathing mode that binds atoms now ignites fusion.

Cosmic web

~10⁶¹ cells. The cosmic web. Still A₁g. Still λ = 0. Still the same shape, 60 orders of magnitude later.
