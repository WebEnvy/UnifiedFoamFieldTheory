Lecture 4 of 8

Making Hydrogen

Six vibrations on one cell
Step 1

The electron

Start with one T₁ᵤ dipole — the electron from Lecture 3. Two hex faces glow pink.

Step 2

Three quarks

Add three more T₁ᵤ modes — one for each quark. Each gets two hex faces. Red, green, blue: the three colours of the strong force.

Step 3

The glue

The T₂g colour lock mode (λ = 7) binds the quarks. Watch the golden shimmer spread across quark faces — that's the strong force holding them together.

Step 4

The binding

Finally, A₁g electromagnetic binding — a white shimmer across all 14 faces. This is the force that holds the electron to the proton.

Hydrogen is six vibrations on one cell. Four particles, two binding forces. Everything you see around you is patterns like this.
