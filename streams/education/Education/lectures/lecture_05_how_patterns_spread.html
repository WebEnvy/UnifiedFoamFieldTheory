How Patterns Spread

Why some forces reach forever and others don't
Step 1

Fermions stay home

T₁ᵤ modes are confined to a single cell. That's what it means to be a particle — you have a location. The pattern activates on the centre cell and goes nowhere.

Step 2

Colour dies fast

The T₂g strong force reaches one cell and stops. That's why you can't feel nuclear force from across the room — it's confined to the proton's neighbourhood.

Step 3

Light goes forever

A₁g has eigenvalue zero — no restoring force opposes it. It propagates indefinitely through the foam. This is light. This is gravity. This is why you can see stars.

Step 4

The weak force barely leaks

Eᵍ acts only on void boundaries (square faces) and barely propagates. It mediates radioactive decay at sub-nuclear distances — the shortest-range force in nature.
