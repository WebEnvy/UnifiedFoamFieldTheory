## The Story

The text calls this *tehom* — the deep. The primordial waters. The undifferentiated abyss.

In UFFT this is the foam before the Big Bang. The same substrate that will produce everything — every particle, every force, every atom, every star, every thought — already exists. It is simply undisturbed.

This is what John means when he writes: *"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."* The law (the Word, the Logos) does not come after the medium — it is coeval with it, inseparable from it. The medium and its law are one thing.

Before the first event: **substrate and law, inseparable, infinite, undisturbed.**
