## 10. What Is Remarkable About the Bahá'í Correspondences

The Bahá'í cosmological claims were made in the mid-to-late 19th century. At that time:

- The Big Bang model did not exist (proposed 1927–1931)
- Quantum mechanics did not exist (1925–1926)
- The expanding universe had not been discovered (Hubble, 1929)
- The concept of a black hole interior as a nested universe did not exist
- The concept of a Planck-scale medium was not formalised

Despite this, Bahá'í cosmology asserted:
- The universe has no beginning (contradicting both religious and scientific consensus of the era)
- Infinite worlds exist within the creation (predating the multiverse concept by ~100 years)
- True void is impossible (contradicting the classical vacuum and anticipating the quantum vacuum by ~50 years)
- Motion is inseparable from existence (anticipating the quantum vacuum energy concept)
- An attractive force arises from "correspondence between realities" (anticipating the geometric/medium interpretation of gravity)

Each of these was heterodox when stated. Each maps onto the UFFT framework. The probability of this convergence by chance, for a text written without access to 20th or 21st century physics, is left for the reader to assess.
