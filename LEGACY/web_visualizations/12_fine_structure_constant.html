Abstract

We derive the electromagnetic fine structure constant α from the geometry of a Planck-scale foam with truncated octahedral (Kelvin cell) structure. The derivation uses no free parameters. Every input is a topological or symmetry integer of the foam cell and its body-centred cubic (BCC) tiling. The result:

α⁻¹ = 8π5/2 × [(|G|−1)/|G| + (V−F)/(d·|G|³) + (E−F)/(d·|G|⁵)]

where |G| = 48 (order of O_h), V = 24 vertices, E = 36 edges, F = 14 faces, d = 3 spatial dimensions. This evaluates to α⁻¹ = 137.035999055, compared to the experimental value 137.035999084 ± 0.021 (CODATA 2018). The discrepancy is 0.21 parts per billion — within 1.4 standard deviations of experiment. This is the first derivation of α from first principles with zero fitted constants.
